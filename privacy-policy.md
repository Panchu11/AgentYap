# Privacy Policy for AgentYap

Last updated: [Date]

## Information We Collect

AgentYap is a Chrome extension that helps generate AI-powered replies for Twitter. We collect minimal information:

### Data We Store Locally
- Your OpenRouter API key (stored in Chrome's local storage)
- Extension preferences and settings
- No tweet content or generated replies are stored

### Data We Process
- Tweet text you select for reply generation
- Your chosen tone preferences
- Generated replies (processed through OpenRouter API)

## How We Use Information

- **API Key**: Used solely to authenticate with OpenRouter for AI reply generation
- **Tweet Content**: Sent to OpenRouter API for processing, not stored by us
- **Preferences**: Used to customize your experience

## Data Sharing

- We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information
- Tweet content is processed by OpenRouter (see their privacy policy)
- No data is sent to our servers

## Data Security

- API keys are stored locally in Chrome's secure storage
- All API communications use HTTPS encryption
- We do not have access to your stored data

## Third-Party Services

- **OpenRouter**: Processes AI requests (see their privacy policy)
- **Twitter/X**: Extension operates on their platform

## Your Rights

- You can delete your API key anytime through extension settings
- Uninstalling the extension removes all local data
- You control what tweets you process

## Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@agentyap.com
